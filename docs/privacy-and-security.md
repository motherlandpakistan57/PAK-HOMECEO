# PAK-HOMECEO — Privacy Safeguards & Domestic Security Architecture

## 1. Domestic Artisan Privacy Protection

In accordance with cultural norms and safety in Pakistan, home-based women artisans must never have their exact residential addresses, phone numbers, or private quarters exposed to public marketplace buyers:

1. **Masked Identifiers**: Master artisans are identified in public catalogs via anonymized artisan codes (e.g. `KB-MLT-402`) and first names.
2. **Doorstep Shielding**: Only the verified local Community Connector holds physical neighborhood delivery coordinates.
3. **Citizen Contact Masking**: Citizen phone numbers and delivery street addresses are masked in artisan views to prevent unauthorized contact.
4. **Consent-Driven Stories**: All artisan craft stories and regional heritage narratives require explicit artisan consent.

---

## 2. Financial Security & Escrow Protection

- **Escrow Settlement**: Citizen payments are locked in an intermediary holding account until the 6-point doorstep physical QC audit passes and delivery is confirmed.
- **Predatory Intermediary Elimination**: Payouts are transferred directly to mobile wallets (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Raast) with zero middleman deductions.
- **Audit Trails**: All transactions, status changes, and batch modifications are recorded in an immutable event ledger (`ledgerEntries` & `auditLogs`).
